CASI Program Descriptions

CASI Research Fellowship

Explore and develop AI safety research projects

Hands-on research experience in AI safety for motivated students who are ready to dive deeper into the field.

Our Research Fellowship provides hands-on research experience in AI safety for motivated students ready to dive deeper into the field. This program requires an extended application process and is designed for students who have demonstrated commitment to AI safety research.

Prerequisites

Applicants must have either completed one of our introductory reading groups with good attendance, or demonstrate equivalent experience in AI safety research or related fields.

Mentorship

Spring 2026 is mentored by PhD students Jennifer Evans and Ida Mattson, with guest mentors brought in by project.

CASI Research Scholars

Structured research opportunities with expert mentorship

Pre-scoped projects that pair undergraduate and masters students with professors, postdocs, and senior PhD students.

What is the CASI Research Scholars program?

The CASI Research Scholars program connects talented undergraduate and masters students with professors, postdoctoral scholars, and senior PhD students to work on cutting-edge AI safety research. This program differs from our Research Fellowship in that research projects are pre-scoped by PhD mentors, allowing you to join a well-defined research initiative with clear direction and support.

How is this different from the Research Fellowship?

While our Research Fellowship is exploratory, where you help define your own research direction through team matching and scoping, the Research Scholars program provides pre-scoped projects mentored by PhD students. This allows for deeper focus and more immediate research engagement.

Application process

Each research project has its own application process. Applications typically require around 10 hours to complete, which may include project-specific assignments, essays, or research proposals. This ensures we find mentees who are genuinely excited about the project at hand.

What you will gain

Expert mentorship

Work directly with PhD students and experienced researchers.

Research skills

Develop real research competencies in AI safety.

Publications

Contribute to research that is concretely directed towards a publication.

Compute and API credits

CASI provides the resources you need to conduct your research.

Impact

Contribute meaningfully to the AI safety research community.

Time commitment

10 to 20 hours per week, varying by project, across collaborative and independent work.
